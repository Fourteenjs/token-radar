How to read this radar

Rank is size; verdict is the farm. The board ranks by implied launch value, dominated by TVL; the verdict prices the farm at the low end (5% of the implied value, shared, minus gas and opportunity cost). A big protocol can be thin when its growth has stalled and its crowd is large. Break-even is the pool share at which the farm nets zero; FARM needs the share at which it nets $200 (11 clear it at 5%, 176 at 15%, 22 never). Growth and age are stated priors, not fitted facts — both were fitted, tested held out three ways, and rejected (see Term tests). Farm steps are templates from category and chains — confirm the market, pool or vault exists before depositing. Verdicts assume the principal comes back on unwind.
