TOKEN RADAR  ·  DEFI LLAMA SNAPSHOT —

Which protocol launches next — and what it would be worth

Every protocol DefiLlama tracks that records no token yet, ranked by the implied value of its launch. The value is this radar’s own claim, built from the snapshot’s own figures: a category’s market price for a dollar of TVL, seven-day growth, and how long the protocol has run with no token. It is not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued.

The model

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

The category multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL of the live-token protocols in that category (at least 3 of them, else the median across all live-token protocols, 0.2138). Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −80…+200% and dampened with a log, weight 0.5. Maturity runs 0.6 → 1.0 over 3 years listed; a protocol with no listing date counts as 1 year old.
