How to read this radar

Rank is size; verdict is the farm. The board ranks by implied launch value, dominated by TVL. The verdict prices the farm: the low-end pool (5% of the implied value) shared among an estimated farmer crowd, minus gas and the yield the capital could have earned elsewhere. A big protocol can be thin when its growth has stalled and its crowd is large. The band is the honest part. Every implied value carries a 90% bootstrap band (200 refits), typically three times wide — neighbours inside one band are a close call. Growth is a stated prior, not a fitted fact: seven-day growth does not predict a richer multiple (correlation −0.14). Two values, never mixed. A pre-token row’s value is the model’s claim; a launched row’s is the market’s own market cap, or the curve’s estimate where none is recorded — and its plan is post-launch usage, not a launch you can still farm. Rows tagged PRODUCT are wrapped, staked, bridged or exchange products: they sit high by TVL because the snapshot counts user balances as TVL. The snapshot carries no users, no revenue and no points program, so the model prices TVL, growth and age only. Farm steps are templates from the protocol’s category and chains — confirm the market, pool or vault exists on its own page before depositing. Verdicts assume the principal comes back on unwind.
