How to read this radar

Rank is size; verdict is the farm. The board ranks by implied launch value, which is dominated by TVL. The verdict prices the farm itself: the low-end airdrop pool (5% of the implied value) shared among an estimated farmer crowd, minus the gas for the whole plan and the yield the capital could have earned elsewhere. A big protocol can be a thin farm when its growth has stalled and its crowd is large; a smaller, fast-growing one can be a farm.

The farmer crowd is an assumption, stated plainly. Farmers grow with the square root-ish of TVL (800 × (TVL / $1M)0.35, between 500 and 120,000): TVL concentrates in a few large deposits, while farmers are counted in users, not dollars.

What the curve cannot see. The snapshot carries no users, no revenue and no points program, so the model prices TVL, growth and age only. A fitted value is typically within a factor of two of the market’s own — useful for ranking, not a price target. The stats above the board say how many pre-token protocols were audited; the Live tokens view shows the 8 largest tokens the curve was fitted on, kept out of the ranking.

Farm steps are templates, not verified listings. Each plan is built from the protocol’s category and chains, so confirm the market, pool or vault exists on the protocol’s own page before depositing. Verdicts assume the principal comes back on unwind, which holds for deposits and vaults but not for insurance premiums, prediction stakes or leveraged positions — for those, treat the verdict as an upper bound.
